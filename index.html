What to compare

Look beyond the price tag.

Check the engine or motor, power, torque, transmission, fuel type, claimed efficiency, seating and safety equipment. The best choice depends on your driving needs, budget and preferred variant.

A car's specifications can vary significantly between variants. Compare like-for-like trims wherever possible.
